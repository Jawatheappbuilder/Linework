How to read this

Compare the reduction in take-home pay, tax saved and possible future balance—not just the amount contributed. The planner assumes the extra amount is salary-sacrificed and deducts 15% contributions tax.

The $30,000 concessional cap includes employer and salary-sacrifice contributions across all funds. This estimate does not include other contributions, carry-forward amounts, HELP/TSL, Medicare levy surcharge, insurance or fund fees. Division 293 may add another 15% tax for higher-income earners. This is a guide, not financial advice.

Compare purchased leave

Each purchased week costs one week of the selected classification's base rate, spread evenly over 26 fortnightly pays.

The workdays setting only converts purchased weeks into extra days off. It does not change the salary calculation.

Assumptions

Purchased leave is treated as a pre-tax reduction equal to the selected weekly base rate multiplied by the weeks purchased. Employer super remains calculated on the full base-rate earnings. Overtime, allowances and the other settings currently entered in the pay calculator remain included.

Actual payroll treatment can depend on your employer's purchased-leave policy, including eligibility, accrual, cancellation and super treatment. Use this as an estimate and confirm the arrangement before applying.

Protection Groups 1–4

1 Normal · 2 Tie · 3 Switching · 4 Special

Group 1

Normal operation

Normal OC, EOC and SEF settings.

Auto-reclose is generally non-auto where cable is included and may need to be manually enabled or disabled depending on the relay.

Group 2

Feeder tie or additional load

Raises the overcurrent setting to allow extra load.

SEF may also be raised when tying feeders with single-phase-to-earth transformers. On a relay with only two available groups, Group 2 may be used for high-speed operation.

Group 3

High-speed overhead switching

SEF blocked, EF raised for single-phase switching and reclosers set non-auto.

Used for authorised overhead, ground-level and glove-and-barrier switching as directed by the applicable procedure and protection information.

Group 4

Special temporary application

Settings vary to suit the specific application and are described on the protection summary card.

Where not specifically nominated, use requires advice or approval from Secondary Systems Planning.

High-speed settings

The source also lists “High Speed Settings” with no group specified. Treat this as a separate instruction—not a fifth group.

The exact group used can depend on the relay and the protection summary card.
